Stride time and stance phase ratio are estimated from a foot-mounted 3-axis accelerometer using filtering, fundamental-frequency windowing, correlation-based turn detection, peak picking and dynamic time warping, achieving a mean absolute error of 0.029 s for stride time and 0.0084 for stance phase ratio against the APDM system.
