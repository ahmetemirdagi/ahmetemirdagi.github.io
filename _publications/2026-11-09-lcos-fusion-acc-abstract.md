LoRA-adapted ECG, PPG and SCG foundation models fused with signal-quality-guided attention to detect LCOS after congenital heart surgery. In 19 prospectively evaluated patients, trimodal fusion reached an AUC of 0.87 (93% sensitivity, 74% specificity), versus 0.59 for a bedside vitals model and 0.57 for a CNN.
