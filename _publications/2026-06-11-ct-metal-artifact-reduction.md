We reframe CT metal artifact reduction as an in-context reasoning task and adapt a general-purpose vision-language diffusion editor with LoRA using only 16–128 paired slices, supplying clean anatomical references from other subjects as exemplars. The approach achieves state-of-the-art perceptual and radiological-feature metrics on the AAPM CT-MAR benchmark.
