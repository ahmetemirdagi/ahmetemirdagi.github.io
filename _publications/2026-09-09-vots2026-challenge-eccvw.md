Results paper of the VOTS2026 challenge, covering four sub-challenges (tracking by segmentation, transformative objects, referring tracking and point tracking). Co-authored as a member of the Codeway team whose Track-On-RS tracker placed 2nd overall and 1st in two of the five accuracy thresholds (d8, d16) in the VOTSp2026 point-tracking sub-challenge.
