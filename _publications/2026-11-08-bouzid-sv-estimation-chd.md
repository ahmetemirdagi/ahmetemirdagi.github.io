Explainable, continuous stroke volume estimation for patients with congenital heart disease from chest-worn ECG and seismocardiogram wearables, using signal quality-aware learning. In 67 patients across two clinical centers and three devices, estimates agreed with cardiac MRI (R² = 0.79).
